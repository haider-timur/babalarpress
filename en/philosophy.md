  <h2>a space for reclaiming our voices</h2>
</div>
<div class="philosophy-text">
<p>Through BABALAR PRESS, we want to create a space for our Qazaq voices, stories, and experiences. For the voices of our near and far, shared and personal ancestors. For training our voice muscles. For realizing and celebrating the value, beauty and power of our voices and stories. For experimenting with, documenting and sharing our voices.</p></div>

<div class="philosophy-visual">
<img src="/assets/images/philosophy-bg-1.png" alt="">
<h2>a learning community and a community of learners</h2></div>
<div class="philosophy-text">
<p>BABALAR PRESS is a learning community – we believe that we all have something to teach and learn from one another. BABALAR PRESS is also a community of learners – this is our first attempt at creating such a space, we are driven by intuition, learning and figuring things out as we go, and accepting-welcoming that mistakes/change/growth will be part of our journey.</p>

<p>As we & people around us learn and reconnect to our ancestral worlds, we are practicing extending kindness to ourselves and one another, and hope you, dear reader and/or potential collaborator, will do the same.</p></div>

<div class="philosophy-visual">
<img src="/assets/images/philosophy-bg-1.png" alt="">
<h2>a space for dialogue</h2></div>
<div class="philosophy-text">
<p>We hope BABALAR PRESS will be an opportunity for us, with our many identities, languages and experiences, to break out of our "bubbles" and come into generative and mutually supportive dialogue.</p>

<p>We aspire to create projects which serve as an intersection for such people, where authors can express themselves and share knowledge in a language and format that suits them, and where they can be heard by “speakers” of other languages.</p></div>

<div class="philosophy-visual">
<img src="/assets/images/philosophy-bg-1.png" alt="">
<h2>our position on language</h2></div>
<div class="philosophy-text">

<p>BABALAR PRESS is a multilingual space. Our forthcoming events, publications & social media posts will all be in Qazaq, Russian and English.</p>

<p>At the same time our team believes in the importance of transitioning to Qazaq as a language that will be spoken and shared by all people within our country, alongside any other languages they may speak. Some members of our community are learning Qazaq language right now, and we invite you all to join us at the time & speed that feels right for you.</p></div>
